1. Introduction

Welcome to Pixel Quest, a mobile color-by-number game developed by Studio De Vooght.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Pixel Quest.

Pixel Quest is intended for a broad audience, including children and adults.

2. Information We Collect

Pixel Quest stores certain information to provide gameplay functionality, including:

  • Completed and unfinished coloring pictures.
  • In-game coins, experience points, and levels.
  • Unlocked content, achievements, and game progress.
  • Game preferences and settings.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

Pixel Quest may also use third-party services that process additional information, as described below.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Pixel Quest integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP addresses and approximate location derived from them.
  • Advertising interactions and performance information.
  • App usage information and diagnostic data.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and support related services.

Advertising personalization and data processing depend on applicable privacy requirements, user choices, and the configuration of the advertising services.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's privacy guidance.

4. Children's Privacy

Pixel Quest is designed to be accessible to players of different ages, including children.

We recognize that children's personal information requires additional protection.

Where advertisements are provided to children, they must comply with applicable child privacy laws and Google Play's Families policies, including restrictions on personalized advertising, data collection, and advertising content.

We do not intend to use children's personal information for behavioral advertising.

Where a player's age or applicable permissions cannot be established, advertising and data processing must be restricted as required by applicable law.

Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or concerns about their child's privacy.

5. Google Play Games Services

Pixel Quest may integrate Google Play Games Services to provide features such as:

  • Player authentication.
  • Achievements and game-related features.
  • Player account integration.
  • Optional game progress synchronization, where available.

When these services are used, Google may process account identifiers, player information, authentication data, and related service information according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Some Google Play Games features may be unavailable depending on the player's account, age, device, or region.

6. Game Progress and Storage

Pixel Quest saves game progress locally to allow players to continue their coloring activities.

Where cloud synchronization is enabled and used, certain game progress information may also be transferred to and stored through Google services.

Removing the application generally removes locally stored game progress, subject to Android backup or restoration features.

Cloud-stored information, where applicable, may require separate management or deletion.

7. Legal Basis and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable data protection laws, personal information is processed on an appropriate legal basis, such as consent, legitimate interests, or the performance of a service requested by the user.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, advertising-related consent choices are provided where required.

Users may withdraw consent or update their privacy preferences through the available privacy controls.

Declining optional advertising-related consent does not prevent access to the core coloring gameplay.

8. Third-Party Services

Pixel Quest may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising and advertising-related services.
  • Google Play Games Services — Authentication and game-related account functionality.
  • Google Play Services — Platform functionality and integration with Google services.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers, including Google, to operate supported game features, deliver permitted advertising, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

The categories of information shared depend on the services enabled and the user's applicable privacy choices.
